# Company Leave Policy (Sample)

Employees accrue 20 days of paid annual leave per calendar year. Accrual
begins on the first day of employment and is prorated for partial years.

Unused annual leave may be carried over into the next year, up to a maximum
of 5 days. Any balance above 5 days is forfeited on December 31.

Sick leave is separate from annual leave. Employees receive 10 days of paid
sick leave per year. A medical certificate is required for absences longer
than 3 consecutive days.

To request leave, submit a request through the HR portal at least 2 weeks in
advance for annual leave. Sick leave should be reported to your manager on
the first day of absence.
